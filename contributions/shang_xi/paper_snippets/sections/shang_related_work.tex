\noindent\textbf{Search cost in context.}
Early-Bird Tickets identify masks early in training~\cite{you2020earlybird}; SNIP prunes at initialization~\cite{lee2019snip}. Structured iterative and one-shot pruning have been studied on tabular data~\cite{bluteau2022tabular}. Our retrospective analysis audits the budget--performance trade-off of the present unstructured IMP protocol, rather than introducing a new pruning algorithm.
